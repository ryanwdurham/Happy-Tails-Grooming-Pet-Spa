Safety First

Keeping Your Pet Safe & Comfortable

🏥

Vaccinations Required

Dogs need current Rabies and Bordetella vaccines. Please bring proof on your first visit to keep all pets safe and healthy.

💧

Hydrate Before You Come

Make sure your pet has had water. Avoid large meals 1–2 hours before their appointment to prevent any tummy troubles.

🧘

Fear-Free Handling Always

We use low-stress techniques and take breaks when needed. We never rush a pet that's anxious or uncomfortable.

📋

Tell Us Everything Upfront

Health conditions, past grooming trauma, behavioral quirks — share it all. No judgment, just better care for your pet.

Our Story

Locally Owned, Pet Obsessed.

Happy Tails Grooming was born out of a simple belief: every pet deserves to feel their absolute best. We started right here in Bothell because this community is home — and we pour that love into everything we do.

Our certified groomers bring years of experience with all breeds, temperaments, and coat types. Whether it's an anxious rescue or a bouncy puppy, every pet gets the time and attention they deserve.

We're not a high-volume factory. We limit daily appointments so every pet gets our full focus. Quality over quantity — always.
